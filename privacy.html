Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how A88 Studios ("we", "us") handles information when you visit a88studios.com or play our games.

Information we collect

  • Game data: scores, progress and settings, usually stored on your own device.
  • Contact info: only if you email us.
  • Usage data: basic information like browser type and pages visited, collected through cookies or similar tools.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads through Google and its partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads based on your visits to our sites and elsewhere on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies.

Some of our ads are optional "rewarded" ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe's handling of your information is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and saved game data in your browser at any time. To ask about or delete any information we hold, email us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new date.
