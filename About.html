About A88 Studios

A88 Studios is an independent game studio making small, fun games you can jump into in seconds, on your phone, in your browser and in VR.

What we make

Quick-play games built around simple controls and that "one more try" feeling. Short levels, fair challenges, and rewards for getting better.

How we keep it fair

Our games are free to play. We keep ads light: most are optional, so you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. No pay-to-win.
